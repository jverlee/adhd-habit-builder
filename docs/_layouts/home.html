What it does

Designed for momentum, not perfection.

Every detail nudges kids forward without nagging. Late still counts. Patterns surface on their own.

Day view

Each habit is a big, colorful card. Tap to complete, or snap a photo as proof — great for "make the bed" or "pack the backpack."

Live score

A live score band shifts from red to yellow to green as the day fills in. Late completions still count for half points — momentum over perfection.

Month view

A calendar where every day is colored by how it went, so patterns surface naturally — no charts, no nagging.

Per-child routines

Each child gets their own habits, photos, and history. Switch between them with one tap.

PIN-locked admin

A PIN gate keeps editing, archiving, and child management out of small hands — so the kid view stays focused.

iPad & iPhone

Native SwiftUI, tuned for big touch targets on iPad and one-handed use on iPhone. Works fully offline.

Privacy by design

Everything lives on the device. Full stop.

No accounts. No servers. No analytics. No ads. No tracking. The app makes zero network calls — your kid's routine is nobody's business but yours.
