# ADHD Habit Builder

A calm, visual routine board for kids with ADHD — built for families, runs fully on your iPad or iPhone, no accounts, no cloud, no tracking.

## What it does

- **Day view.** Each habit shows up as a big, colorful card. Tap to complete, or snap a quick photo as proof (great for "make the bed", "pack the backpack", "brush teeth").
- **Live score.** A red / yellow / green band fills as the day goes on. Late completions still count for half points — the goal is momentum, not perfection.
- **Month view.** A calendar where every day is colored by how it went, so patterns surface without nagging.
- **Per‑child routines.** Each child gets their own habits and history.
- **Admin gate.** A PIN‑protected admin area to edit, archive, or delete habits and manage children.

## Privacy

Everything lives on the device. No accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. The app makes zero network calls.
