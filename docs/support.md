# Support — Habi

_Habit builder for kids with ADHD._

Thanks for using Habi. This page is the best place to get help, report a bug, or share feedback.

## Get help or report a bug

The fastest way to reach us is to **[open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/jverlee/adhd-habit-builder/issues/new/choose)**. You don't need a paid account — a free GitHub account is enough.

When reporting a bug, please include:

- What you were trying to do
- What you expected to happen
- What actually happened
- Your device model and iOS version (Settings → General → About)

Screenshots or a short screen recording are very helpful.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does the app work offline?

Yes. Habi is **fully offline**. It makes zero network calls and works the same in airplane mode as it does on Wi‑Fi.

### Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts, no sign‑in, no email required. All data is stored on your device.

### How do I back up my data?

The app's data is included in your standard iCloud or device backup automatically. We don't run a backup service of our own — if your device is backed up, the app's data is backed up with it.

### How do I delete a child's data?

Open the admin area (PIN required), tap the child, and choose Delete. Removing the app from your device deletes everything.

### Can I use this on multiple devices?

The app currently stores data per‑device. There is no sync between devices.

### Is this app free?

Yes — there are no ads, no in‑app purchases, no subscriptions, and no upsells.

### Where's the privacy policy?

See the [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}).

## Feature requests

We'd love to hear what would make the app more useful for your family. [Open a feature request on GitHub](https://github.com/jverlee/adhd-habit-builder/issues/new/choose) and describe the use case.
